The legendary Tata Sierra has been revived as a premium 5-seater mid-size SUV. The internal combustion engine version starts at 11.49 lakh INR, offering 1.5-litre petrol and diesel powertrains alongside a new all-wheel-drive configuration. Following closely, the flagship Tata Sierra EV starts at 18.79 lakh INR, built on the acti.ev+ platform with 63kWh and 75kWh battery packs that yield up to 665 km of range. Both iterations feature a signature retro-modern boxy silhouette with classic Alpine windows, a triple-screen TheatrePro dashboard, a 5-star Bharat NCAP safety rating, Level 2+ ADAS, and an immense 622-litre boot space.
