India's electric car market is evolving rapidly but lags significantly behind global leaders like China and the West. Globally, electric passenger cars dominate the EV landscape, driven by robust infrastructure and high consumer adoption. In contrast, India's EV revolution is powered by two- and three-wheelers rather than four-wheelers. Electric cars face slower adoption in India due to high upfront costs and a nascent charging network. However, government initiatives like the PM E-Drive scheme and growing domestic manufacturing are narrowing the gap. While the world sprints ahead in electric cars, India is carving its unique, multi-modal path toward electrification.
