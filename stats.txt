Choosing the better vehicle depends entirely on whether you prioritize family practicality or futuristic performance. The Tata Sierra EV stands out as the ideal family SUV, offering a spacious boxy cabin, exceptional rear headroom, a massive 622-litre boot, and a dual-motor quad-wheel drive setup that hits 0-100 km/h in 5.8 seconds. Conversely, the Mahindra BE 6 wins on range and tech. Its sharp coupe design offers up to 683 km of range from a 79 kWh battery, a fighter-jet cockpit, 282 BHP rear-wheel-drive handling, and a reassuring lifetime battery warranty.
