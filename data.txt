Ultraviolette Automotive is India's premier electric two-wheeler company for high-end performance, but not for mass market sales. If your priority is cutting-edge engineering, aggressive styling, and exceptional top speed, UV's flagship F77 Mach 2 motorcycle stands completely unrivaled with its industry-leading 323 km range. Conversely, if 'best' implies practical daily commuting, affordability, and widespread service networks, legacy giants like TVS and Bajaj dominate. Together with Ather Energy, they control over eighty percent of India's volume market share. Ultimately, Ultraviolette reigns supreme in the premium sports segment, while TVS and Ather remain superior choices for everyday commuter scooter buyers seeking value.
